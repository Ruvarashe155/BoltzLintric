ABOUT BOLTZ LINTRIC

WHO WE ARE

A trusted partner in engineering and construction.

Boltz Lintric Enterprises (Pvt) Ltd is a Zimbabwean civil engineering and construction company focused on delivering high-quality infrastructure solutions.

Our work spans civil engineering, structural engineering, construction, transportation infrastructure, water and drainage systems, and land development.

We combine technical knowledge, practical experience and responsible construction practices to deliver projects that are safe, durable and fit for purpose.
